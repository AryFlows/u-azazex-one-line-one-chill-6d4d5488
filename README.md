## The entry

> I finally matched the handwriting in the margins of my diary — it is mine, dated three years from now.

102 characters, one sentence, inside the 1–200 gate.

## Why this line

The scare is earned by what it withholds: the diary's margins carry warnings, the handwriting is the writer's own, and the dates are three years ahead — so the dread is not a monster in the room but a future self already trying to warn the present one. Concrete and specific (handwriting, margins, diary), no mirror, doll or basement, and the last words — "three years from now" — land the blow.
